# The Last Plot -  A HARVEST THEMED GAME!

---

## A 2d godot pixelated game, where you play as a farmer in a tropical country that is suffering from a famine, and YOU are the only farmer surviving.

---

### Features:
	
* **Custom pixel art**: Made in Asperite - tiles, icons and ui elements
* **Enemy pathfinding**: Uses GDscript to implement mechanisms such as enemy pathfinding
* **Harvest-themed**: Themed on the harvest season

---

### The game is based around you, a farmer in a tropical country suffering from a famine, with you being the only farmer remaining in whole nation.
### You must survive and thrive across day-night cycles, by reaping crops at day and resisiting beasts at night, which are after your crops.

--- 

## Enemies:
* **Dog**: Gnaws and defecates at your crops, ruining them
* **Skeleton**: Damages your crops via brute force
* **Slime**: Slimes your crops, rendering them useless.

--- 

## Powerups:
* **Power**: boosts your SLASH STRENGTH
* **Lower cooldown**: Makes your slashes reload faster

---

### To run locally, simply clone the repo and open in godot 4 engine
